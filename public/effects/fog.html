Fog

A bathroom mirror that just fogged up. Clear it any way you like.

  • Breatheon your screen, or hold Space, to fog the glass
  • Pinchthumb to index finger to draw in the fog
  • Fistwipe the fog away. Or just drag with the mouse or a finger.

Everything runs in your browser. No frames, audio or data leave this machine.
